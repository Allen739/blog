# Random Thoughts

> These are thoughts written for myself over time. They are unfinished, sometimes contradictory, sometimes emotional. They are not conclusions, just moments. I'll probably turn each part a blog of itself in the future.

# On Decisions

> *Written: June 27*

Lately, I have been thinking that maybe a life of regret is still a life that can be lived.

I've accepted many of my decisions, my laziness, my drifting away from things I once cared about, and even my own feelings. Instead of constantly wishing I had chosen differently, I've found myself wondering if accepting the consequences is enough.

Maybe peace isn't found by erasing regret.

Maybe it is found by learning to live beside it.

# On Movies

I have realised I don't watch movies for the thrill.

I watch them for the feeling.

Every now and then, a character experiences something that somehow mirrors my own thoughts, and for a brief moment it feels like someone else understood something I never knew how to explain.

I don't chase excitement.

I chase that feeling.

# On Sadness

This year, I realized something strange.

I've been trying to experience sadness rather than avoid it.

Not because I enjoy suffering, but because sadness feels honest.

Some songs...
Some movies...
Some late nights...

They remind me that being human isn't only about happiness.

# On the Purpose of Life

People often ask,

> "What is the purpose of life?"

I'm not even sure that's the right question.

At one point I started reading about history and how people lived.

Eventually I came across stories like the Rwandan genocide.

Children who had only just begun speaking...
Families that disappeared overnight...
Lives that ended before they even had the chance to begin.

And there has been more dark chapters of our world like this and still happening....

And I remember asking myself:

**What was their purpose?**

The more I thought about it, the less that question made sense.

So here's what I believe.

I don't think life comes with a purpose waiting to be discovered.

I think we're simply here to experience it.

Joy.

Fear.

Love.

Regret.

Hope.

Heartbreak.

None of that means we should passively accept everything that happens to us.

It simply means that when life places us inside a moment, whether it's a beautiful movie, a late-night song that unexpectedly makes us cry, or a conversation we'll never forget, we should allow ourselves to truly experience it.

That feels enough.

# Why tell Strangers these things

Sometimes I wonder tell complete strangers the deepest parts of myself.

I think everyone, at least once, wants someone to simply listen.

Not to solve anything.

Not to ask why.

Just to know that another person witnessed a piece of their existence.

# On Dreams

There was a time when I felt like I had completely given up.

Nothing interested me anymore.

Responsibilities felt unbearable.

I wanted to stay in bed.

I wanted to disappear from expectations.

More than anything, I felt as though I was constantly running out of time, that if I failed, I'd disappoint everyone around me.

It was one of the lowest places my mind had been.

Looking back, I don't know whether I wanted to quit life.

I think I just wanted the pressure to stop.

# On Suicide

One night I found myself thinking about why some people end their own lives.

People often look from the outside and say,

> "It could have been solved."

Maybe that's true.

But I don't think the important question is whether the problem was solvable.

The important question is whether, in that moment, anyone truly saw the world the way that person was seeing it.

Understanding someone's pain doesn't always mean fixing it.

Sometimes it begins with trying to see it through their eyes.

I love telling my friend whenever i have a problem that i'd rather have them listen and say nothing that just straight up blabbering the solution to it.

Listening is a very powerful superpower you can have.

# On Heartbreak

Sometimes I imagine conversations that never happened.

Maybe because they help me understand emotions before life asks me to live through them.

One of them ends quietly.

No shouting.

No dramatic speeches.

Just two people saying goodbye.

And somewhere between those final words is the realization that some endings don't need villains.

Sometimes they are simply endings.

# On Attention

Sometimes I ask myself:

> Why do you want anyone to read these thoughts?

The honest answer is...

I don't know.

Maybe nobody cares.

Maybe that's true.

But writing has never really been about attention.

It's about leaving evidence that, at one point, these thoughts existed.

# A Small Reminder

If things don't work out...

that's okay.

They were never guaranteed to.

Enjoy the small moments while they're still small.

Don't hold onto resentment longer than you need to.

Life has a strange way of continuing.

# On the Person I Want to Become

I've been thinking about creating my own philosophy for living.

Not becoming someone emotionless.

Not becoming someone who doesn't care.

But becoming someone who remains steady regardless of what life brings.

Someone who:

- doesn't waste energy complaining,
- accepts reality quickly,
- responds with discipline instead of anger,
- continues improving physically, mentally, emotionally, and intellectually.

Strength, to me, isn't pretending not to feel.

It's feeling everything...

and still choosing the next step.

I don't know how long this sentence will survive.

Maybe I'll fail.

Maybe I'll forget I even wrote it.

But today, I believed it.

And today is enough.
